Our Story

Independent since 2015.

At The East Agency, we pride ourselves on being more than just an insurance provider - we're your neighbors, your advocates, and your trusted advisors. Locally owned and operated since 2015, we've been serving Cartersville and the surrounding communities for over a decade, and our commitment to personalized service runs deep.

Being independent means we're not stuck selling one company's policies - we shop dozens of top-rated carriers to find the coverage that actually fits your life, at a price that makes sense. Whether it's the truck in your driveway, the boat at the lake, the home you're raising your family in, or the business you've built from scratch - we treat it like it's ours.

If you're not thrilled with your current insurance, let's talk. No pressure, no sales pitch - just an honest conversation about what you actually need.

The People, Not Just The Policies

Who you're actually talking to

No call centers, no scripts read off a screen. Just the two of us, and a whole lot of stuff hanging on the office walls that doesn't belong there.

Brannon East

Founder

"If it ain't fun, I don't want to do it."

I started The East Agency in 2015 because I wanted an agency that actually felt like Cartersville: straightforward, a little rough around the edges, and genuinely glad you walked in. Walk into my actual office and you'll find old gas cans hanging from the ceiling, a full-size stoplight, bourbon barrels, and car parts scattered around. Licensed in 11 states, but Cartersville's always been home. When I'm not at the office, I'm usually somewhere with my family: in the kitchen cooking up something with wild game, on a hiking trail, out on the water, or chasing big Eastern turkeys through the woods.

Mary Evans

CSR & Licensed Agent

"Same smile, whether you're in the office or on the phone."

Mary lives in Taylorsville, GA, and she's a licensed agent for auto, home, life, and health insurance. Same care, same smile, whether you catch her in the office or on the phone. She's been married 31 years, has 3 kids and 6 grandchildren, and when she's not chasing a good thrift store find, she's out at the drag strip cheering her husband on. Her own pride and joy sits in the garage: a 1969 Dodge Dart she's not letting go of anytime soon.
