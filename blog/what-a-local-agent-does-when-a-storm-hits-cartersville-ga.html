What a Real Local Agent Does When a Storm Hits Your House

By Brannon East · November 24, 2026 · 4 min read
← Back to Blog

Honestly, we don't see a lot of claims come through our office, and I think that's by design. When someone buys a policy from us, we take the time upfront to explain what a claim actually is, how it works, and just as important, what it doesn't cover. When people understand their coverage before anything happens, there are a lot fewer surprises later. But every now and then, something happens that reminds me why this job is about more than just writing a policy.

A storm, a tree, and a client who shouldn't have been alone

A few years back, a bad storm rolled through our area. One of our clients, a widowed mother, had a large tree come down right on her property. I got the call, and my wife and I drove out there ourselves.

We met the tree removal crew when they arrived. We helped move furniture out of the way so the cleanup crew could work. We pitched in where we could, because that's what you do for your neighbors. Then I stayed with her through the entire claims process, start to finish, all the way to the last repair. That included meeting the insurance adjuster at her house in person so she didn't have to explain everything or negotiate anything on her own.

She shouldn't have had to go through that by herself. To me, that's what being somebody's agent actually means. It's not just selling a policy and hoping you never hear from them again.

Why most claims never need to go this far

I want to be clear that most claims aren't dramatic. Most of the time it's a lot simpler: a fender bender, a hail-damaged roof, a pipe that burst while someone was traveling. But even on the simple ones, the same principle applies. If you understood your policy when you bought it, filing a claim isn't confusing or scary. You already know what's covered, roughly what to expect, and who to call.

That's actually one of the reasons claims are rare for our clients. We don't just hand over a policy and move on. We walk through it together so there's no guessing later. When people know what they're actually buying, they tend to need less hand-holding when something goes wrong, because nothing catches them off guard.

Why it matters who you buy from

Here's the thing a lot of people don't think about until they need it: when you buy insurance online from a call center or a national brand, the person who sold you the policy usually isn't the person who shows up when something bad happens. You might talk to five different people before your claim gets resolved, none of whom have ever met you.

When you work with a local independent agent, that's not how it works. I live in Cartersville. My kids play sports here. If a storm comes through Bartow County, it's hitting my community too, not some call center three states away. When one of our clients needs us, we're close enough to actually show up.

That's not a sales pitch, it's just the honest difference between buying a policy and having an agent.
