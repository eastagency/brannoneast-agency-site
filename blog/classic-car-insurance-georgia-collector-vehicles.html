Classic car insurance Georgia: Protect your collector vehicle

August 31, 2026 · By Brannon East

Why standard auto insurance won't cut it for your classic car

If you own a classic or collector vehicle, you already know it's not like your everyday sedan. That 1967 Corvette in your garage, the restored pickup truck you've spent years perfecting, or that vintage Mercedes you finally found at an estate sale, they all deserve protection that actually makes sense for what they are.

Most standard auto insurance policies are built for cars that depreciate and get driven to the grocery store. They don't account for the real value of a collector vehicle, the specialized restoration work you've done, or the fact that you're probably not racking up 12,000 miles a year on it. If something happened to your classic car under a regular policy, you'd likely find yourself underwater on a claim, fighting with an adjuster who doesn't understand what your vehicle is actually worth.

What classic car insurance in Georgia actually covers

Collector vehicle insurance is fundamentally different because it recognizes what you've actually invested in your car. When you insure a classic car, the policy can be built around an agreed-upon value that you and your insurer settle on together, based on appraisals, market data, and the condition of your specific vehicle.

That means if something happens, you're not stuck trying to prove value after the fact. You already know what you'll receive. Most classic car policies also let you keep your vehicle in a garage or climate-controlled storage, which lowers your risk profile and your premium. Some policies even cover roadside assistance specifically for collector vehicles, spare parts coverage, and towing to a shop that actually knows how to work on vintage cars.

Here in Cartersville and throughout Bartow County, we see folks with everything from muscle cars to foreign imports, and each one needs a different approach. A 1970 Chevelle has different risks than a British sports car from the 1950s. Your insurer should understand those differences.

How to get the right rate on your collector vehicle

You'd think finding the right coverage for a classic car would be simple, but most big insurance companies treat them like regular cars or won't insure them at all. That's where shopping matters. At The East Agency, we work with more than 20 different insurance carriers to find the ones that actually specialize in collector vehicles and offer rates that make sense for your situation.

When you call us about insuring your classic car, we ask the right questions. How often do you drive it? Is it stored in a garage? Has it been professionally appraised? Do you have any modifications or restorations we should know about? Have you done any recent work to bring it back to original specs? All of that affects your rate, and we know which carriers will give you credit for the responsible stuff you're already doing.

The carriers we partner with understand that classic car owners are different. You maintain your vehicles better. You drive them more carefully. You keep detailed records. That discipline should be reflected in your premium, and it is when you're working with the right company.

Getting your classic car insured with us

Whether you've got a car that's been in your family for 40 years or a recent acquisition you're restoring, we know how to get you set up with a policy that actually fits.

Your classic car represents more than just money. It's a piece of automotive history and probably a lot of your own time and passion. It deserves insurance coverage that recognizes that value, and we're here to make sure you've got it.
