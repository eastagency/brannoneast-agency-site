Life insurance for young families in Georgia

August 3, 2026 · By Brannon East

Why life insurance for young families in Georgia matters right now

Look, I talk to young parents all the time who think life insurance is something they'll get to eventually. Maybe when they buy a house. Maybe when they get a promotion. But here's the reality I've seen in Cartersville and throughout Bartow County: the younger you are when you lock in coverage, the cheaper your premiums are going to be. Full stop.

Think about it this way. If something happened to you tomorrow, could your family stay in the house? Could your spouse take time off work to raise the kids without drowning in debt? That's what life insurance actually does. It's not morbid to think about it. It's responsible.

How much coverage do you actually need

Most young families I work with need somewhere between 500,000 and 1.5 million dollars in coverage. The exact number depends on your mortgage, your income, how many kids you have, and what you want for their future. Some people want to cover college. Others want to make sure the mortgage is paid off.

Here's a simple rule that tends to work: take your annual income and multiply it by ten. That's a starting point. If you make 50,000 a year, you're probably looking at coverage around 500,000. If you make 80,000, you'd want to think about 800,000. But these numbers aren't set in stone. We can talk through your specific situation and figure out what actually makes sense for your family.

Term life insurance is usually the move for young families

You've probably heard about different types of life insurance. Term life and whole life are the two main ones. For young families, term life insurance is almost always the way to go.

Here's why. Term life is straightforward. You pick a term (usually 20, 25, or 30 years), you pay a fixed premium every month, and your family gets the death benefit if something happens. Because it's simple and the insurance company's risk is limited to that specific timeframe, it's cheap. Really cheap. We're talking maybe 30 to 50 bucks a month for solid coverage if you're young and healthy.

Whole life insurance is more complicated and costs a lot more. It covers you for your entire life and has a cash value component. It's not bad insurance, but for most young families just starting out, term life does what you need at a fraction of the cost.

Getting the best rate isn't hard if you know where to look

Here's something that surprised me when I got into this business. The same person can get quoted three completely different prices from three different insurance companies. Exact same person. Exact same coverage. Totally different costs.

That's why we shop 20+ carriers when we're finding coverage for someone. We're not loyal to any one company. We're loyal to our clients getting the best deal. Some carriers like young families. Some specialize in people with health issues. Some just happen to offer better rates that week. The point is, you don't want to just call one company and take their number.

At The East Agency, we handle the heavy lifting. We pull quotes from multiple carriers and show you what's actually available. You pick what works for you.

The application process is easier than you think

Most young families worry the application is going to take forever or require a bunch of medical tests. Honestly, for most people it's pretty painless. You answer some health questions, they might ask for your medical records from your doctor, and that's usually it. No blood tests or anything crazy.

Some policies are even simpler. Certain carriers offer what they call simplified issue or guaranteed issue policies where you skip a lot of the medical underwriting altogether. Your premium might be slightly higher, but you get covered faster and easier.

The whole process from first conversation to coverage in place usually takes two to three weeks, depending on how quick you get your paperwork back to us.
