Why Bundle With The East Agency

Most of Brannon's clients are not just insuring one thing - it's a home, a couple of cars, and usually a boat, ATV, or golf cart too. Instead of filling out a separate quote for each one, the bundle conversation asks for your information once and carries it forward - if your vehicles are kept at the same address as your home, you will not be asked to type that address in twice.

One Conversation, Everything Covered

Just tell us what you'd like a quote for - home, vehicles, specialty items, or all of it - and the conversation walks through each one in order, asking only what's relevant. At the end, Brannon reviews everything together and shops 20+ carriers to find the combination that actually saves you the most, since the "cheapest bundle" from one company is not always the best deal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get one quote for my home, cars, and boat together?

Yes. The East Agency's bundle quote walks you through everything you want covered in one conversation - your home, your vehicles, and specialty items like a boat, RV, ATV, or golf cart - without making you repeat information like your address twice.

Does bundling my insurance actually save money?

Often, yes - many carriers offer multi-policy discounts when you insure your home and auto (and sometimes specialty items) together. Brannon shops 20+ carriers to find the combination that actually saves you the most, rather than assuming one bundled carrier is automatically cheapest.
