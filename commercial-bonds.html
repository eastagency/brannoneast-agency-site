What Is a Commercial Bond?

Commercial bonds are license, permit, and compliance bonds that businesses and individuals need to legally operate in a specific role, separate from construction/contract bonds and court bonds. They're required by state agencies, county offices, or regulators as a condition of doing business, and they protect the public and the regulator rather than the person getting bonded.

Vehicle Title Bonds in Georgia

A vehicle title bond (also called a bonded title or lost title bond) comes into play when you can't produce a clean, transferable title for a vehicle, trailer, boat, or similar property. This happens most often when a title is lost, was never properly transferred, or the vehicle is older or home-built and never had one. The Georgia DMV can require this bond before issuing you a new title, and it protects anyone who later shows up with a legitimate prior claim to the vehicle.

Georgia title bond amounts are typically based on the vehicle's appraised value, and smaller bonds often carry a flat minimum premium. Brannon can walk you through exactly what the DMV will need from you to get the bond issued and your title process moving.

Other Common Commercial Bonds

Notary Bond: Georgia doesn't require a standard surety bond as part of notary commissioning, but some notaries carry one anyway, or get asked to by a specific employer, bank, or title company. We can help if that's you.
Auto Dealer Bond: required for licensed new and used motor vehicle dealers in Georgia.
Lottery & Retailer Bond: required by the Georgia Lottery Corporation for convenience stores and retailers selling lottery tickets. See our dedicated Georgia Lottery Retailer Bond page for details.
Sales & Use Tax Bond: sometimes required by the Georgia Department of Revenue for certain businesses to guarantee tax payments.
Utility Deposit Bond: an alternative to a cash deposit with a commercial utility provider, guaranteeing the bill gets paid.
ERISA / Employee Benefit Plan Bond: required of anyone who handles funds for certain employee benefit plans.

How Much Does a Commercial Bond Cost?

Most commercial bonds cost a small percentage of the required bond amount per year, often with a flat minimum premium for smaller bonds. The exact rate depends on the specific bond type, the amount set by the regulator or agency, and your personal credit profile. Brannon confirms the exact bond amount required for your situation, then shops surety markets for your best rate.

Commercial Bond vs. Contract Bond

Commercial bonds are ongoing compliance requirements tied to your license or role, and they typically renew annually. Contract bonds are tied to a specific construction project and expire when that project closes out. If you're not sure which applies to you, Brannon can help you figure it out quickly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a commercial surety bond?

A commercial surety bond is a broad category covering license, permit, and compliance bonds that businesses need to legally operate, separate from construction/contract bonds and court bonds. Common examples include notary bonds, auto dealer bonds, vehicle title bonds, and lottery retailer bonds.

What is a vehicle title bond?

A vehicle title bond, sometimes called a bonded title or lost title bond, is required by the Georgia DMV when you can't produce a clean, transferable title for a vehicle, often because it's lost, missing, or the vehicle was acquired without one. The bond protects anyone who later turns up with a legitimate ownership claim.

Do I need a bond to become a notary in Georgia?

Not for standard commissioning. Georgia does not require a surety bond to become or renew a notary public commission. Some notaries carry one voluntarily, or because a specific employer, bank, or title company asks for it. If that applies to you, Brannon can help you get bonded.

Do convenience stores need a bond to sell lottery tickets in Georgia?

The Georgia Lottery Corporation can require retailers to post a surety bond before issuing a license to sell lottery tickets, with the amount based on your store's sales volume. See our dedicated Georgia Lottery Retailer Bond page for details.

How much does a commercial bond cost?

Most commercial bonds cost a small percentage of the bond amount, often with a flat minimum premium for smaller bonds. Pricing depends on the specific bond type, the amount required, and your personal credit.

Does it cost more to use an independent agent instead of buying direct?
